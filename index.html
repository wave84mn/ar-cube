AR Cube

Hold the printed cube (or lay the paper card on a table) in front of your phone. The building appears on top of it and turns with it.

Paper card + paper cube (PDF) Cube print file (3MF) Cube STLs (zip)

Works in Safari on iPhone and Chrome on Android. Good, even light helps; avoid glare on the cube. Nothing leaves your phone: the camera image is processed on the device.

How to use

  1. Print the cube (two colours: white body, black squares), or print the paper card / paper cube from the PDF at 100 %.
  2. Hold the cube 20–50 cm from the phone in good light. Keep at least one face fully visible.
  3. Turn the cube to turn the building. Tap a model below to switch.
  4. If the building jumps, avoid glare on the cube and hold it a little closer.
